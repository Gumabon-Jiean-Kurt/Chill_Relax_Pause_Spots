About Escolta

Escolta is a historic district in Manila known for its colonial architecture, shopping, and vibrant street life. It is a great place to explore the city's rich history and experience its unique culture.

For students, Escolta can be a great place to explore, shop, and experience the local culture without needing to travel far from the city.

Why Students Should Visit

  • Enjoy the view of Manila Bay
  • Good place for an evening walk
  • Relaxing atmosphere
  • Great place to watch the sunset
  • Accessible from different parts of Manila

What You Can Do

🌅 Watch the Sunset

Enjoy the famous Manila Bay sunset while relaxing along the waterfront.

🚶 Take a Walk

Walk along the bay and enjoy the open surroundings with your friends.

📸 Take Photos

The waterfront provides a nice background for photographs and memories.
