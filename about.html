About Manila Student Guide

Welcome to the Manila Student Guide! This website was created to help students discover interesting places around Manila where they can hang out, relax, explore, and spend time with their friends.

The guide focuses on places that are accessible and enjoyable for students. Each place has its own page containing useful information such as its location, opening hours, price range, and the best time to visit.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make it easier for students to find places to visit without spending too much time searching for information. Whether you want to relax, take photos, explore history, or simply spend time with friends, this guide can help you find a place that suits you.

Featured Place

One of the featured places in this guide is Rizal Park, a historic and relaxing destination located in the heart of Manila.
