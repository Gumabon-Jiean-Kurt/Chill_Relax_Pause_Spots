Places for Students to Hang Out and Relax in Manila

Rizal Park (Luneta)

Rizal Park is a spacious and peaceful place where students can relax, walk around, enjoy the scenery, and spend time with friends. It is also a great place to take a break from school without spending much money.

Escolta

Escolta is a historic area known for its old buildings, unique atmosphere, and growing creative community. Students can visit to explore, take photos, discover interesting places, and hang out with friends.

Manila Baywalk

Manila Baywalk is a popular place to relax while enjoying views of Manila Bay, especially during sunset. It is a simple and accessible spot where students can walk, talk with friends, and unwind after a busy day.
